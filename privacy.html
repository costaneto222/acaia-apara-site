Privacy in Acaiá Apará

Version of October 30, 2026.

In short

  • The game never asks for a name, email, phone number, photo or location.
  • No ads, no selling of data and no tracking outside the game.
  • There is a single purchase, to support the game, made through your device's store. It gives no in-game advantage.
  • The ranking and the crash reports use the internet.
  • You can delete your online account and turn off the crash reports at any time.

What stays only on your device

  • Your progress: finished levels, drops, records, stamps and options.
  • The log of your runs and the ghost fruits.
  • None of this leaves the device, with one exception: when you challenge a friend, the fruit's path in that run goes along, to show as a ghost for them.

What goes to the ranking server

  • A random code the game creates for your device. It is not your phone number and does not say who you are.
  • Your nickname, an animal and a quality from a fixed list, such as "Swift Thrush".
  • Your best runs: the level, the time, the lives, the drops, the points, the game version and the date.
  • Your friend code and your friendships, when you add a friend.
  • The challenges you send or receive, with the fruit's path of the challenging run.

What it is for

  • Showing the ranking of Brazil and of each state.
  • Checking that the points are real and blocking too many submissions.
  • Connecting friends and challenges.

Where it is kept and for how long

  • The server is run by Supabase, in the São Paulo region, in Brazil.
  • The data is kept while the account exists.
  • An account unused for 12 months is deleted automatically, with all its runs.

How to delete

  • In the game, open Options and tap "Delete my account".
  • This deletes your nickname, runs, friendships and challenges from the server.
  • Your progress on the device stays. To erase it, use "Erase progress" in Options.

When the game crashes

  • If the game closes by itself or freezes, it sends an automatic report, so we can find out what happened.
  • The report carries: the device model, the system and its version, the game version, the error and which screen or level the game was on, with the game's last actions (changing screens, starting or finishing a level).
  • It does not carry your name, email, phone number, location, a picture of the screen, your device's code or your friend code. The IP address is not kept.
  • The service Sentry receives it, on servers in the European Union. Each report stays for 30 days and then goes away.
  • To turn it off: the "Send crash reports" switch in Options.

When you support the game

  • The Golden Seed is a one-time purchase, made and charged by your device's store (Google Play or App Store).
  • The game never sees or stores a card, an address or any payment data. That stays with the store.
  • On the device, the game only keeps whether you own the Golden Seed or not. None of it goes to the ranking server.
  • "Already bought" asks the device's store whether the purchase exists, to give the Golden Seed back after a reinstall.
  • The purchase sits behind an age question and an adult confirmation. Only the chosen age band (up to 11, 12 to 17, 18 or more) stays on the device, so the store knows which button to show; it is never sent.
  • Refunds are requested in the store itself (Google Play or App Store), under its rules.

Children

  • The game is for all ages.
  • It asks for no personal data, has no chat and no free text.
  • Friends only join through the friend code.
